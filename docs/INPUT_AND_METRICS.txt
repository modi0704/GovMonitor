GOVMONITOR - HOW TO ENTER THE PRACTICE INPUT AND READ THE RESULTS
==============================================================
Use SAMPLE_INPUT.txt beside this file. Work on a computer.
The figures are for learning. Use approved figures for official reporting.

1. SIGN IN AND PREPARE
Sign in with your own agency administrator account.
Open User guide in the left menu for the full screen-by-screen instructions.
If first-time setup appears, complete the agency's level names and office setup.

In a learning copy, create the office PRAC - Practice Office under the agency.
Use Structure, select the parent office, then Add office.
Give office staff access through Users if another person will enter the report.
An agency administrator can perform this entire learning exercise.

2. CREATE THE PRACTICE YEAR
Open Fiscal years > New fiscal year.
Set Label to Practice 2026 and Start year to 2026.
Use January as the first month of the agency's fiscal year for this example.
Choose Excel method and create the year.
Open the year's scoring settings. Keep weights by cost, no ceiling, and
zero on-time margin. For this exercise, leave automatic locking off and
supporting files optional. Save settings if you changed them.
Select Open for the year and confirm.
These settings explain the expected figures below; other settings can change them.

3. ENTER THE ANNUAL PLAN
Open Plans. Choose Practice 2026 and find PRAC.
Select Start empty plan. Open the draft if the editor does not open automatically.
On Activities, set New row to Output and select Add at end.
Fill OUT-01 and its title. Leave its quantity, cost, and month cells empty.
Select that row. Set New row to Activity, then Add below selected.
Fill ACT-01 using PLAN ROW 2 in SAMPLE_INPUT.txt.
Add another Activity below ACT-01 and fill ACT-02 using PLAN ROW 3.
Keep both activities under OUT-01. Use Quantity for both measures.
Select PRAC as the implementing office for both activities.
Enter plain numbers without PHP or a percent sign.
For ACT-02, leave Unit cost empty and enter Total cost as exactly 40000.
Total cost is the amount used to calculate its weight.
The monthly totals must equal 12 sessions and 120 cases respectively.
Leave Weight for the app to calculate: ACT-01 is 60%, ACT-02 is 40%.
Leave Indicators empty for this exercise.
Select Save and resolve any listed problems.
Select Submit for approval and confirm.

4. APPROVE THE PLAN
Open Approvals, select Practice 2026, and Review the PRAC plan.
Check the three rows, target totals, monthly schedule, and costs.
Return to Approvals. Under Budget vs planned cost, find PRAC, enter 100000
in Approved budget, and select Save beside that amount.
In the approval queue, select Approve for PRAC and confirm.
The plan should say Approved before monthly entry starts.

5. ENTER JANUARY
Open Monthly report. Choose PRAC, Practice 2026, and January 2026.
Use the office's own plan.
For ACT-01 enter 1 in Done this month.
For ACT-02 enter 8 in Done this month.
Copy the January remarks and the delay reason from SAMPLE_INPUT.txt.
Leave actual spending empty for this exercise.
Select Save and wait for Saved, then select Submit report and confirm.
For official reports, attach genuine proof whenever required by your agency.

6. REVIEW AND FINALIZE JANUARY
Open Submissions. Choose Practice 2026 and January 2026.
Open report for PRAC and check the quantities and delay explanation.
If a correction is needed, Return the report with a clear note, correct it,
save it, and submit again.
When every report for the month has been reviewed, select Lock January 2026.
Lock applies to the entire agency's selected month, not just PRAC.
Confirm only after checking the list. This is why the exercise needs a learning copy.

7. ENTER FEBRUARY
Repeat the reporting steps for February: ACT-01 = 1 and ACT-02 = 12.
Enter only February's completed work, including the two cases finished late.
Do not enter 2 sessions and 20 cases; those are the two-month totals.
Save, submit, review, then lock February after January is locked.

8. READ THE DASHBOARD
Choose PRAC, Practice 2026, and the month you are checking.
Use the office's own plan, with offices beneath it, when that choice appears.

THIS MONTH
The share of the entire year's weighted work completed in this one month.
It is not the share of the month's workload completed.
January: 7.67%. February: 9.00%.
The badge compares this figure with the month's plan.
February's badge is +0.67 pp vs plan: 9.00% completed against 8.33% planned.

TO DATE
The share of the year's weighted work completed from January through the
selected month. January: 7.67%. February: 16.67%.
The February change badge is +9.00 pp vs last month.
The small line shows recorded history; a missing figure is not a zero.

VARIANCE
Completed progress to date minus planned progress to date.
January: 7.67% minus 8.33% = -0.67 percentage points.
February: 16.67% minus 16.67% = 0.00 percentage points.
A percentage point is the difference between two percentages.
For example, moving from 40% to 45% is a gain of 5 points.

STATUS
With the example's zero margin: negative = DELAYED, zero = ON-TIME,
positive = ADVANCED. Your agency may choose other words or a margin.
January is delayed. February is on time after catching up on both cases.

FUNDS USED (ESTIMATE)
This exercise has no recorded actual spending. The app estimates funds used
from the overall progress and the PHP 100000 budget.
January: PHP 7666.67 used, PHP 92333.33 remaining.
February: PHP 16666.67 used to date, PHP 83333.33 remaining.
These are estimates, not proof of payments. If actual spending is recorded,
read its label carefully; the displayed basis can change.

DELIVERY TREND AND BUDGET OVERVIEW
Green line = completed work. Dashed gray line = planned work.
Completed work below plan means the office is behind its planned pace.
The budget ring shows the share used. View as table gives the exact figures.
View as chart returns to the picture.
By quarter summarizes quarter totals. Quarter details opens the full numbers.

WHY THE ACTIVITIES COUNT DIFFERENTLY
ACT-01's PHP 60000 cost gives it 60% of the office score.
ACT-02's PHP 40000 cost gives it 40%.
January score = (1 / 12 x 60%) + (8 / 120 x 40%) = 7.67%.
February total = (2 / 12 x 60%) + (20 / 120 x 40%) = 16.67%.
The office score does not simply average the two activity percentages.

RANKINGS AND FINALITY
Rankings compare only the offices in the selected scope and group.
One practice office alone does not provide a useful comparison.
FINAL means the month is locked. PROVISIONAL means figures may still change.
Saved means entries were kept. Submitted means sent for review.
Neither Saved nor Submitted means the month is final.

9. DOWNLOAD THE RESULT
On Dashboard, choose the office, year, and final month first.
Select Export > Excel workbook. Choose PRAC only and the intended month.
Select Prepare workbook. Wait for Ready in the dialog or My exports.
Select the filename to download it. Check the month and figures in the file.
Sign out when finished.

IF YOUR FIGURES DIFFER
Check office, month, year, plan version, and scoring settings first.
Confirm costs are 60000 and 40000 and monthly targets sum to annual targets.
Check that January entries were not entered again in February.
Check whether the funds card uses an estimate or recorded spending.
Do not change official scoring rules merely to reproduce a practice result.
