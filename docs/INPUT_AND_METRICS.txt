GOVMONITOR - PRACTICE: TYPE IN THE FIGURES AND CHECK THE RESULTS
===============================================================
Use the figures in SAMPLE_INPUT.txt. Work on a computer.
These figures are for practice only. Use a practice copy, not your official year.

Sign in with your administrator account. The app opens on "Step-by-step".
Follow the six steps below. The progress bar shows how far you are.


STEP 1 - ADD THE OFFICE
Go to step 1 and press "Open the offices".
Select your top office, then press "Add office":
   Code: PRAC     Name: Practice Office
Press "Back to the steps" in the green bar.


STEP 2 - SET UP THE YEAR
Go to step 2 and press "Add the year" (or "Open the years").
Press "New fiscal year".
   Label: Practice 2026
   Starts in the year: 2026
   Scoring method: Excel method
Press "Create", then press "Open" next to Practice 2026 and confirm.
At the top of Step-by-step, choose Year: Practice 2026.


STEP 3 - ENTER THE PLAN
Go to step 3. Next to PRAC press "Start plan".
On the plan page:
1. Set "New row" to Output and press "Add at end".
   Code: OUT-01   Title: Community services delivered
   Leave the numbers empty on this heading row.
2. Select that row. Set "New row" to Activity and press "Add below selected".
   Fill in PLAN ROW 2 from SAMPLE_INPUT.txt (ACT-01).
3. Add another Activity below ACT-01 and fill in PLAN ROW 3 (ACT-02).
   For ACT-02 leave Unit cost empty and type 40000 in Total cost.
Type plain numbers only (no PHP sign, no % sign).
Jan to Dec must add up to the Target: 12 sessions and 120 cases.
Leave Weight empty. The app works it out: ACT-01 60%, ACT-02 40%.
Press "Save". If problems are listed, fix them and save again.
Go back to Step-by-step and press "Mark as final" next to PRAC.
PRAC now shows "Final".


STEP 4 - ENTER JANUARY
At the top of Step-by-step choose Month: January 2026. Go to step 4.
Next to PRAC press "Enter results".
   ACT-01  Done this month: 1
   ACT-02  Done this month: 8
   ACT-02  Reason for delay: copy it from SAMPLE_INPUT.txt
Copy the remarks too. Leave "Amount spent" empty.
Press "Save", wait for "Saved", then press "Submit report" and confirm.
PRAC now shows "Done".


STEP 5 - CHECK AND CLOSE JANUARY
Go to step 5 and press "Open the dashboard". Check the figures (see below).
If a figure is wrong, go back to step 4 and correct it.
When it is right, press "Close January 2026" and confirm.
Closing makes January final for the whole agency. That is why you practise
in a practice copy.


REPEAT FOR FEBRUARY
Choose Month: February 2026 and do steps 4 and 5 again:
   ACT-01  Done this month: 1
   ACT-02  Done this month: 12
Type only what was done IN FEBRUARY (including the 2 late January cases).
Do NOT type 2 and 20: those are the totals; the app adds the months for you.


STEP 6 - DOWNLOAD
Go to step 6. Press "Monthly report", choose "Excel workbook",
"Practice Office only" and the month, then "Prepare workbook" and "Download".
The file also stays under "Downloads" in the menu.


WHAT THE RESULTS SHOULD BE
                     January        February

Planned to date      8.33%          16.67%

Status               DELAYED        ON-TIME
Funds used           PHP 7,666.67   PHP 16,666.67
Funds remaining      PHP 92,333.33  PHP 83,333.33

What the cards mean
- This month: the share of the WHOLE YEAR's work done in this one month.
- To date: the share of the whole year's work done from January up to now.
- Gap: to date minus planned to date. Below zero means behind plan.
- Status: below zero = DELAYED, zero = ON-TIME, above zero = ADVANCED.
- Funds used: an estimate from the work done and the PHP 100,000 total cost
  (because no actual spending was typed in).

Why ACT-01 counts more than ACT-02
ACT-01 costs PHP 60,000 so it is 60% of the office's score.
ACT-02 costs PHP 40,000 so it is 40%.
January = (1 / 12 x 60%) + (8 / 120 x 40%) = 7.67%
February to date = (2 / 12 x 60%) + (20 / 120 x 40%) = 16.67%
The score is NOT the simple average of the two activities.

FINAL means the month is closed. PROVISIONAL means it can still change.


IF YOUR FIGURES ARE DIFFERENT
- Check the office, year and month at the top of the page.
- Check the costs: 60000 and 40000.
- Check that Jan to Dec add up to 12 and 120.
- Check that you typed the month's figure, not the running total.
- Do not change the scoring settings to make the numbers match.
